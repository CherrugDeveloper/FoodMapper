# Decoding the FODMAP Acronym

## Summary

Molecular breakdown of fermentable short-chain carbohydrates.

## FERMENTABLE

Not a specific component.

Describes the ability of intestinal bacteria to ferment unabsorbed carbohydrates and generate gas.

## OLIGOSACCHARIDES

### Fructans

- Wheat
- Rye
- Garlic
- Onions

### Galactans (GOS)

- Beans
- Chickpeas
- Lentils

Humans lack the enzymes required to digest them.

## DISACCHARIDES

### Lactose

Composed of:

- Glucose
- Galactose

Requires the enzyme lactase for digestion.

## MONOSACCHARIDES

### Fructose

Commonly found in:

- Apples
- Pears
- Honey

Absorption mainly relies on GLUT-5 transporters.

## POLYOLS

Include:

- Sorbitol
- Mannitol
- Xylitol

They are slowly absorbed and may increase osmotic water attraction.

## Conclusion

FODMAPs are not inherently harmful.

In IBS patients they may increase:

- Bloating
- Gas production
- Abdominal pain
- Bowel habit alterations

through a combination of fermentation, osmotic effects and visceral hypersensitivity.
